I am a researcher and post-doc in Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. I got my PhD degree in Computer Sicence from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/). I am very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Zhao](https://lvpgroup.github.io/) of LVP Lab (Logic, Verification, and Programming Lab) from Zhejiang University. In my fouth year of PhD, I went to [University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign (UIUC)](https://illinois.edu/) as a visiting PhD student under Professor [Talia Ringer](https://dependenttyp.es/)'s guidance. Before that, I received a BS in Physics (Honors Program) from [Xi'an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/). I also went to Michigan State University as an exchange student to learn physics.

My research interests include AI, formal verification, automatic theorem proving, large language models, operating systems, trusted execution environments, and specification synthesis. I am now interested in FM4AI, AI security, and agents. Before that, I verified the TEE and its protection profile with Isabelle/HOL, automatically specified and verified the system security and safety (e.g., Trusted execution environment) in Isabelle/HOL with the assistance of LLMs.
